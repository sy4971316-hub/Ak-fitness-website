# 🏋️ AK Fitness (एक फिटनेस) — 24/7 Premium Gym Website

A mobile-first, high-conversion website for **AK Fitness**, South Delhi's premier round-the-clock fitness centre located at 370 Hospital Road, Bhogal, New Delhi.

---

## 🌟 Key Features
- **24/7 Interactive Dial**: Visual analog clock with hour slider & presets to explore gym vibe & crowd levels across all 24 hours.
- **Before/After Transformation Slider**: Touch & drag comparison slider.
- **Hourly Crowd Meter**: Dynamic busy-hours bar graph (Weekday vs. Weekend).
- **Interactive Reviews Carousel**: 4.6★ rating with 475+ verified Google review stories.
- **Filterable Gallery with Fullscreen Lightbox**: High-res machinery, free weights, and cardio decks.
- **Trial Pass & Instant WhatsApp Generator**: Interactive form validating entries and generating pre-filled WhatsApp passes.
- **Mobile Floating Action Bar**: 1-click Call, WhatsApp, and Trial booking.
- **Local SEO & Schema Markup**: JSON-LD `HealthClub` schema with address, coordinates, 24/7 opening hours, and aggregate rating.
